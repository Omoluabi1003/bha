# BHA Staff Training Blueprint

BHA staff training will use the production workflow itself. Training should feel like doing the job with guidance, not attending a software class.

## Training structure

Each role receives short scenario-based modules. A module has five parts: purpose, guided task, safety check, independent repetition and completion confirmation.

### Admissions
Scenario: A parent has submitted an application.

The staff member learns to locate the application by name or BHA reference, verify the applicant, place an incomplete decision under review, record the final decision, and understand that approval does not itself create an invoice.

### Academics
Scenario: A staff member needs to work with an assigned student or class.

The staff member learns to establish the correct context, complete the assigned academic action, review critical values and confirm the saved result.

### Finance
Scenario: A payment or invoice requires review.

The staff member learns to locate the correct financial record, verify identity and amount, complete only the permitted action and confirm the resulting balance/status.

### CBT Operations
Scenario: A student needs operational help during an examination session.

The staff member learns to identify the correct session, use permitted session information, recover from supported operational interruptions and return control to the examination engine without seeing answer keys.

## Competency

Completion is based on demonstrated tasks, not time spent reading. Staff should be able to repeat the workflow independently and explain the safety boundary that applies to their role.

## Product implication

Future operational screens should support contextual help, plain-language status, clear next actions, safe confirmations, useful empty states and recoverable error states. Technical backend language should never be necessary for routine school operations.

## Later rollout

When production dashboards are mature, convert these scenarios into in-product guided walkthroughs and a staff onboarding checklist. Training records can then track role, module, completion and refresher needs without granting additional operational permissions merely because a module was completed.
