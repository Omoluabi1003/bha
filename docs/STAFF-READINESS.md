# BHA Staff Readiness Standard

## Product principle

**Powerful underneath. Simple on the surface.**

BHA staff should be trained to perform their school responsibilities inside BHA. They should not be trained to operate Supabase, inspect database tables, run SQL, understand APIs, or troubleshoot application infrastructure.

## Role-first training

Training is organized around work, not technology:

- Admissions: receive, locate, review and disposition applications.
- Academic staff: work with permitted classes, students and academic records.
- Finance: review invoices, payments and balances without changing admissions decisions.
- CBT operations: support sessions without access to protected answers.
- Proprietor/authorized leadership: oversight, approvals and exceptional actions.

## Interface standard

Every operational screen should answer four questions without a manual:

1. Where am I?
2. What needs my attention?
3. What action am I allowed to take?
4. What happens after I take it?

Use plain school language. Avoid database terminology in staff-facing UI. Prefer visible status, search, filters, confirmation, success feedback and recovery guidance over technical error messages.

## Safety standard

Role permissions are enforced by the server/database, not merely hidden buttons. Destructive or financially significant actions require deliberate confirmation. Examination answer keys remain unavailable to operational users who do not require them. Admissions submission never automatically creates a financial obligation.

## Training rollout

When BHA is ready for staff onboarding, create short scenario-based modules for each role. A staff member should be able to complete a real workflow during training using the same screens used in production. Training material should use screenshots and tasks such as: find an application, place it under review, approve it, locate a student, review a payment, or support a CBT session.

The target is operational confidence for a competent smartphone user without requiring technical knowledge.
