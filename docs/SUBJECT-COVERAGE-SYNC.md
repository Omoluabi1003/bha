# Subject Coverage Synchronization

The BHA index and CBT BHA Internal catalogue now consume the same `bhaAcademicSubjects` source. The 18 subjects are therefore accounted for in both surfaces:

English, Oral English, Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Economics, Government, CRS, Literature in English, Commerce, Accounting, Geography, Computer Studies, Practical Biology, Practical Physics and Practical Chemistry.

CBT aliases route the BHA display names to their verified core banks where appropriate, including Oral English, Further Mathematics, CRS, Literature in English, Accounting and the three practical sciences.
