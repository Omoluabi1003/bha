# Question Sourcing Policy

Research JAMB, WAEC and NECO official structures, syllabi, objectives and legitimately published samples to understand expected scope and style. Use those sources as specifications for original BHA questions.

Do not scrape and republish entire copyrighted past-question booklets or commercial question-bank websites. BHA should own its authored practice content and keep provenance/review notes for imported material that is legitimately licensed or public-domain.
