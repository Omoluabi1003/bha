# Subject Coverage Acceptance

- The homepage and BHA Internal CBT catalogue use one shared 18-subject source.
- Oral English is represented in WAEC, NECO and BHA Internal.
- Practical Biology, Practical Physics and Practical Chemistry are represented in WAEC, NECO and BHA Internal.
- Further Mathematics and Computer Studies are represented in BHA Internal and relevant SSCE catalogues.
- BHA naming aliases resolve to an appropriate verified bank instead of appearing unavailable because of wording differences.
