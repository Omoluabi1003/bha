# CBT Bank Capacity

Published numbered sets require real inventory.

Examples:

- 10 JAMB Mathematics sets require 400 distinct approved questions.
- 10 JAMB Use of English sets require 600 distinct approved questions.
- 10 fifty-question practice sets require 500 distinct approved questions.

Until that inventory exists, BHA publishes only the complete sets the approved bank can support. Remaining numbered selectors stay locked as Bank in Review. This is intentional and follows the GeoAware-Core no-fake-completeness standard.
