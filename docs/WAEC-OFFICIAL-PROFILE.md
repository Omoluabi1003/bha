# Official WAEC 2026/2027 profile used by BHA

For 2026 and 2027, WAEC Nigeria states that candidates can sit English Language and General Mathematics as the two available core subjects and select six or seven additional subjects to reach eight or nine total subjects. Citizenship and Heritage Studies Education and Digital Technologies are identified as new core subjects but are not to be examined until 2028.

BHA therefore does not add those two deferred subjects to the 2026/2027 WAEC CBT picker.
