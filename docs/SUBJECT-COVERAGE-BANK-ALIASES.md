# Bank Aliases

BHA display subjects reuse the appropriate verified core bank where the distinction is presentation or practice focus rather than a separate objective bank:

- English / Oral English → English bank
- Further Mathematics → Mathematics bank for current foundation coverage
- Practical Biology → Biology bank
- Practical Physics → Physics bank
- Practical Chemistry → Chemistry bank
- CRS → Christian Religious Studies bank
- Literature in English → Literature-in-English bank
- Accounting → Accounting bank

Dedicated practical and advanced banks can replace these aliases later without changing the shared subject catalogue.
