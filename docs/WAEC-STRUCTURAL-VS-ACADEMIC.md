# WAEC readiness terminology

**Structurally ready** means the official-current picker catalog is represented correctly, a subject has enough validator-accepted questions to assemble its configured CBT sitting, and question IDs do not collide across WAEC subjects.

**Academically certified** requires question-level syllabus mapping, answer verification and reviewer evidence, plus prescribed-text/version evidence where applicable.

BHA must not use these terms interchangeably.
